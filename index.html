FAQ

Zero-surprise answers.

How long does shipping take?

Most orders ship in 3–7 days from our shop in Woods Cross, Utah. Current ship window is shown at checkout, and you’ll get tracking by email the moment it leaves the warehouse.

I already have a post — do I need the bundle?

No. Keep your existing post and add the $40 mailbox bracket adapter at checkout instead of the full post bundle. No new concrete, no new post. A wall-mounted version is also available if you’d rather skip the post entirely.

Are the house numbers included?

Not in the base mailbox price. The 4-inch address numbers are magnetic, anodized aluminum, and sold as a 4-number set for $13 — snap them on, no tools needed.

Can I lock it?

Yes. Add the locking mechanism for $30 at checkout for extra peace of mind on mail and small packages.

What if I don’t love it?

Free 30-day exchanges. If the color or size isn’t right, we’ll make it right.

Who actually makes this?

A small team in Woods Cross, Utah, founded by Danny & Veronica. Every Malone is hand-finished in our own warehouse — not drop-shipped from a catalog.
